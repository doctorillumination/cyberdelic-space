What the sources state

Art Blocks documents generative scripts, deterministic token hashes, and dependencies stored on Ethereum. Its on-chain generator can assemble browser-viewable HTML from that data without relying on Art Blocks servers. Its open-source core contract manages token ownership, project scripts, token hashes, and royalty settings.

What the Cartographer infers

Art Blocks and FN-33 share an inquiry into generative publication that can survive the disappearance of its original venue. They are not equivalent. Art Blocks uses Ethereum, ERC-721 tokens, minting, and protocol contracts. FN-33 proposes Bitcoin parent-and-pull lineage, plate striking, public copyability, and a marketplace kept subordinate to the press.

This is a proposed relation, not a claim of endorsement, partnership, or architectural equivalence.
