# Opalia

A fold in seeing. Light, continually becoming.

Pearlescent waves pass through a continuous generative field. Their contours breathe while the colours separate, overlap, and return. Open Controls to change the rhythm, seed, spacing, contour, or iridescence, or leave the surface to unfold.

Opalia invites attention without a task. Repetition becomes a way of noticing difference, rather than a demand to arrive somewhere. Its relation to Cyberdelic OS lies in that encounter with perception and time. This is a curatorial reading, not a claim about consciousness or a physical simulation of light.

By Brad Necyk. Previously developed as Phase Loom. This living artwork is not inscribed.
