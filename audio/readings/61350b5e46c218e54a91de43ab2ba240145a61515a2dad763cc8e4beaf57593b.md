# Small weather

A garden pool holds the blue of the sky and the shade of leaves. Rain passes through the reflection in a field of keyboard characters. As the ripples spread, small flashes of colour catch along their crests.

Click or tap to send a ripple across the pool. Turn on Sound for the soft patter of rain, or let the water move in silence.

By Brad Necyk, 2026. A living artwork from Cyberdelic OS.
