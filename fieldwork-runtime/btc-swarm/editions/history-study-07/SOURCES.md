## Editorial distinctions

- The crisis is context for Bitcoin, alongside earlier cryptographic work. A single motive is not attributed to Satoshi.
- The genesis headline is identified as embedded coinbase data. Its byte range is highlighted in both columns. The header hash is verified locally.
- The whitepaper expressions depict the section 11 model under p > q. They are not a universal security guarantee.
- Satoshi’s April 2011 departure line comes from an email to Mike Hearn. It is not described as a final blog entry.
- Len Sassaman is included as a cypherpunk and privacy advocate. No person in the portrait set is identified as Satoshi.
- The earlier gold logo treatments are redrawn studies. The orange mark is a public-domain vector derived from the community design.
- Mt. Gox quantities describe the company’s March 2014 report, including rediscovered coins. They are not represented as a final adjudicated theft total.
- Bitcoin Cash and SegWit are placed inside the scaling dispute. The fork diagram is a schematic, not a complete chain genealogy.
- The halving schedule is a protocol rule. A repeating price cycle is an interpretation.
- MicroStrategy’s quantities are the August 2020 announcement, not current holdings.
- US regulatory conflict includes criticism and the administration’s investor-protection rationale. Product approval is distinguished from approval of the protocol or a prediction of returns.
- The reserve order is described with its initial forfeiture funding. It is not represented as an unrestricted new-purchase program.
- October 2025 liquidation estimates concern crypto derivatives collectively. The SIFMA document is an industry submission hosted by the SEC, not a finding by the SEC. Longer-term market-maker damage and a bear-market origin remain interpretations.
- Price plots use historical Coin Metrics observations through October 10, 2025. Monthly points are the last available observation in each month; October is partial. The detail plot uses daily observations. No current market value is displayed.
- Rainbow bands use an original retrospective log-time regression on monthly data from 2012 onward. Their placement is a visual heuristic and does not predict prices.
