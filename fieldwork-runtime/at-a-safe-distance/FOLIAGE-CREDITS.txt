UNDER GLASS / BOTANICAL ASSETS

The foliage and flower sources below are from Poly Haven and released under CC0.
They are included locally with their photo-based PBR textures.
Prepared and packed in Blender for this artwork. Geometry and texture
source metadata is preserved in reference/foliage-sources/; raw download caches
remain in the original local workspace.
