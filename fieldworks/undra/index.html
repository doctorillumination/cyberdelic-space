Undra

Living artwork · Not inscribed

A living surface. A conversation with water.

Hold empty space to grow a droplet. Drag water together, let it gather, then separate it again. The field responds through a procedural model of fluid motion and light. Its forms are generated in the browser, not played from a film.

Undra offers a small experience of agency without complete control. Your gesture changes the conditions, and the material answers in its own way. This connection to Cyberdelic OS is a curatorial interpretation, not a claim that the artwork implements the whole operating system.

By Brad Necyk. Previously developed as Glop, also known as Water Droplet. This living artwork is not inscribed.

Hold to grow. Drag to gather. Double-tap to separate.
