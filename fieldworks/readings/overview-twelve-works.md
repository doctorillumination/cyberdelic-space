# Fieldworks

Generative artworks and interactive environments by Brad Necyk.

A surface that answers. A fold in seeing. An unfinished sky. Impossible matter. A river remembering a drawing. A small vocabulary, an expanding field. A field of competing futures. A picture with weight. A room that keeps trying to sing. A history in formation. A manual, held differently. A drawing that keeps swimming.

Twelve living artworks. Enter, explore, and see what a gesture changes.
