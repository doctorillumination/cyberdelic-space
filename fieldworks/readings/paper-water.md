# Paper Water

An ink pond, alive to a small gesture.

Fish drift across a field of textured paper. Their bodies hold different amounts of ink: fine contours, soft grey washes, dark patches. Leaves float at the edges. Shapes below the surface fade into the page.

The fish move through their own rhythms of swimming, coasting, and small bursts of speed. They sometimes gather and sometimes wander apart. Click or tap to drop a few blots of food. Nearby fish approach, rise, and take small bites. A mouth opens, a ripple spreads, and the pond begins to settle again.

Paper Water holds a drawing in motion. Its depth comes through changes in definition, pigment, and the slight distortion of the water. The viewpoint stays still while the life within it continues.

By Brad Necyk, 2026. This living artwork is not inscribed.
