# Aeria

An unfinished sky. A little weather of your own.

Draw a cloud with a mouse, finger, or pen, or choose Make a cloud. Change the light, invite rain, and allow the formations to drift, overlap, evaporate, and gather again. You can pause Life to keep a still moment.

Aeria begins with a gesture, then makes room for something to continue beyond it. It offers a small, playful encounter with emergence and impermanence. Its weather is an artistic model, not a meteorological simulation, and its connection to Cyberdelic OS is an interpretation rather than a complete implementation.

By Brad Necyk. Previously developed as Clouds and Cloudroom. Your sky stays in this browser. This living artwork is not inscribed.
