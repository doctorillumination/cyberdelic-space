# Primitives

A small vocabulary, an expanding field.

Circles, crosses, rings, and repeated marks gather in a grid. Each shape is flat and translucent. Where the inks overlap, a darker colour appears. A complex design can be only two or three simple forms sharing the same center.

Constellation opens the work as a field of individual marks and small gatherings. Scroll or pinch to move between large shapes and a distant composition of fine, distinct cells. In Pattern lab, stepped islands gather, break into fragments, or dissolve into scattered cells. Empty space travels with the marks.

Cyber motion guides the view through smooth changes of scale while individual rows and columns shift one cell at a time. Pause to hold a composition, remix it, or save an image. Optional hand zoom lets you move through the field with two hands: apart for a wider view, together to approach. Hand tracking runs locally in your browser, and the camera starts only when you choose it.

By Brad Necyk. This living artwork is not inscribed.
