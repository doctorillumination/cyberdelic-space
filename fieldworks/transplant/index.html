Transplant

Living artwork · Not inscribed

A manual, held differently.

Transplant began during Brad Necyk's postdoctoral fellowship at York University. Page by page, he poetically rendered the manual given to patients before a liver transplant. Its headings and instructions become a structure to think and feel within.

Science explicates; poetry implicates. Columns become parallel voices. Indents open distances between bodies. A sentence can break apart, interrupt another, or disappear beneath a cancellation.

The web edition holds thirty-three pages. Ribbon red, blue, and green meet dark marker and the pale fibres of worn paper. Ink pools, bleeds, and drips. Some impressions remain defined; others seem rubbed away. Clear passages share a page with partial erasures, and no two leaves weather in the same way.

Turn a leaf across the spine. Its stained reverse stays beside the next poem, with a faint trace of the writing showing through. Read slowly. Turn back. Let the spaces hold part of the account.

Poetic manual by Brad Necyk. Web edition, 2026. This living artwork is not inscribed.

Pull the right page leftward to turn. Pull the left page back. Use Closer to read the fine impressions.
