BTC / SWARM

Living artwork · Not inscribed

A history in formation.

Hundreds of cursors inhabit a black desktop. They open folders, move windows, copy passages, carry images, and reserve spaces for things that have not arrived. Behind their work, a website keeps assembling and scrolling. No single cursor holds the whole design.

The material is Bitcoin history: financial collapse, the white paper, a newspaper headline inside the genesis block, early exchanges, lost coins, arguments, institutions, and recurring dreams of a different kind of money. Archival photographs meet diagrams, verified bytes, and clearly labelled imagined scenes. Sources travel with the fragments. Uncertainty remains visible.

Open a folder. Drag a piece of the page. Gather the swarm and watch it break apart. The desktop is a fiction, but its coordination happens here, through local algorithms. Every seed produces another arrangement. The archive keeps becoming an interface, and the interface keeps becoming a draft.

By Brad Necyk, 2026. This living artwork is not inscribed.
